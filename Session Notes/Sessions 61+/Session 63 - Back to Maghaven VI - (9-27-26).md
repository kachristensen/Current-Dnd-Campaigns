## IC Synopsis - Valindra
>The sting of pain began to subside from the encounter with the large slime creature. After an hour of rest, my skin finally felt as if it was normal again. What weighed more on me was the scolding Persephone had given both me and Rem as we fought the beast.
  As we prepared to depart I wanted to speak to her. Try to find out why there was so much animosity between her and Rem and how to keep the peace. Persephone more or less told me that she and Rem will forever be at odds. That as their goals currently align that is all that keeps things bound.
  I tried to offer to Persephone to perhaps swallow her pride and be the one to keep things functional. Her response that thing have been of late with her being the one swallowing pride and that there is not much more she can or willing will roll over for.
  After said attempt we pressed on. Coming first to a waterfall we found some scaled creatures in the water. The reminded me of the crocodiles we found in the forest before. Aggressive as they were, they were put down fast. Just around the bend we found a quite friendly and forward rat. Using her magic, Persephone spoke with the vermin who bid us to follow. Though she gleaned that it was more of luring than following. 
  We came upon what only could be described as a rats nest. Some large and small swarms. As they showed they were going to attack we struck first. Persephone used a powerful insect swarm and wiped out many of the smaller rats first. The larger ones were more durable and one of them fell to my glaive. As we pressed to wipe out these creatures a larger rat, a king, appeared. Just as we set up to go after it, a small swarm of rats all began to speak in out minds. The uncanny feeling of being spoken to in my mind was an uncomfortable feeling. Though these rats seemed far more intelligent and willing to make a deal. First we offered the slain crocodiles as food and the king accepted and allowed us passage. 
  We learned from the rats that they had a deal with the humans, Grub's humans, to keep the sewers safe from the goo people. Which seemed to have been that Red had expanded themselves to the rest of the sewers and a flooding of sewers helped the rats gain territory. 75% of the sewers held by the rats and 25% held by the goo people.
  Around the corner we were attacked by a large creature with hooks for hands. As we pressed the attack it's wail paralyzed many of us. As we stand stunned, its allies attacked us with bows from the shadows. After shaking off the stun I went and finished off the beast. As I made the final attack, we glimpsed perhaps something of a sadness or grief in the eyes of it as it fell. The remaining enemies went down fast enough without their larger ally.
  Coming to the surface we come to Maghaven proper again. The city much different than before. It is much quieter than before. People do not seem to be about the city. What we do see are patrols of creatures and guards. They seems to be abominations. Twisted forms from who knows that. Much like the one with hooks for arms. What horrors have come to this city after we left?
  Quickly we climbed to the rooftops. Once a patrol passed by, I used my magic to conceal us better, as we went between more houses and streets. To our sheer luck we were able to make it to the prison without being seen. Entering the prison we found patrols of creatures here too. Under the veil of my magic we were able to stay hidden as Ria sent out a spider familiar to look about the prison. Having found where we believe Sabine to be just as her spider was attacked by some construct we began to move into making our way to save her.

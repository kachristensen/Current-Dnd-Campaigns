## IC Synopsis - Lewis
>The Battle of Maghaven Prison  64
  We planned a decisive strike, hoping to eliminate as many guards as possible before raising the alarm. Persephone slipped ahead under the cover of invisibility. We waited in tense silence, catching faint whispers of her enchanting words before she surged into the darkness and struck the first guard unconscious. For a brief moment, we held the advantage, but it didn’t last long. The remaining guards quickly caught wind of our presence, and the prison erupted into chaos.
  Valindra struck the next guard, and Rem swiftly knocked him unconscious. Rem then confronted the captain of the rebel soldiers while the nonhuman forces began their assault. Arrows and shots flew through the air, some striking Persephone while others glanced harmlessly off my armor. A Gem Stalker descended upon Rem, battering him relentlessly, but Persephone commanded the beast to its knees, momentarily turning the tide. Meanwhile, Valindra charged the next Gem Beast approaching from the north and somehow turned the creature into an ally. It teleported into the water, where it struck an Angler lurking beneath the surface.
  Our reprieve was short-lived. A golem charged through the prison bars, forcing Rem to swing at the hulking construct, though his strike seemed to do little more than tear away some of its hair. An Invoker appeared alongside a massive beast wielding enormous needles, adding yet another threat to the growing battle. Rem and Annica charged forward to meet them, while Persephone desperately tried to save the guard caught in the chaos. Ria unleashed a fireball against the golem, and I stepped forward to strike the construct myself, refusing to show fear in the face of its overwhelming strength.
  The battle grew even more desperate as the frozen guard finally broke free. Rem and Persephone were both struck by a devastating lightning bolt, leaving our position increasingly precarious. Then the fighting turned against me. Amid the clash of steel, the roar of monsters, and the crackle of magical energy, I suffered a massive blow. Our carefully planned ambush had become a full-scale battle, and Maghaven Prison was proving far more dangerous than we had anticipated.

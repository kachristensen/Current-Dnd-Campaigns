## Notes
- River
	- Found a river, a woman bathing in it. She called to us, seemed surprised. 
	- Perse did not trust her, commanded and smacked her upside the head nonlethally with her lute. Started combat. The wake she moved in the water seemed twice her size. Illusion. 
		- More giant snake naga ladies with big boobs approached us from her scream. We told them to stand down and retrieve her sister, but they demanded compensation and said in abyssal they would make quick work of us. One ran off screaming murderers.

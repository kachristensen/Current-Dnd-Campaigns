## Summary


## IC Synopsis - Ria
> Following our brief respite, we resumed our trek southward to meet with Lord Aaron. Little was to be had the way of discussion among the party in the wake of the frustrating end to our encounter with the cave dragon. Rembrandt and Ulrich did chat amongst themselves, however. 
   Further along the road, we encountered the carcass of an elk, and paused to investigate. No sooner had we stopped than we were ambushed, Ulrich taking a crossbow bolt from someone hidden up a tree. 
   We engaged with our attackers, rushing to meet them at their uphill position. Persephone was temporarily downed by the same sharpshooter who first struck Ulrich. Meanwhile, a mage on their side conjured a wall of ice to separate Valindra and Ulrich from the rest of us. After healing Persephone through Annica, Rembrandt joined the others on the other side of the wall, while the presumed leader of our attackers called orders to take us one at a time, beginning with Ulrich. 
   Over time we were able to regroup on the other side of the ice wall and begin felling those who ambushed us, beginning with a priest and their berserker. Rembrandt gave chase to the assassin, while the rest of us focused on the remaining assailants.
   As more of their number fell, their captain attempted surrender. However, Persephone remains largely disinterested in accepting such, and killed their mage before moving in on the captain himself, to Ulrich’s displeasure. Valindra followed Persephone’s lead, and attacked the captain as well. In the moment, even Lewis found little mercy left in his heart, calling for what he called repentance. 
   Rembrandt incapacitated the remaining priest and the assassin, and the captain made his last stand, although he did seem to hold back. Likewise, Valindra incapacitated the captain rather than killing him outright. 
   Persephone moved on to relieving the dead and unconscious of their weapons, while Lewis went searching for means of identifying their group. They were, to no surprise, of the rebel faction, although what all has changed since our last intervention has yet to be seen in full. 
   Ulrich left before the engagement concluded, but returned within about ten minutes with his flask in hand.  We gathered together under Persephone’s dome to rest once more, while Lewis cast a healing prayer.
   When the group was sufficiently recovered, Lewis made to wake our erstwhile prisoners, beginning with the priest. Rembrandt joined him to speak with the rebels. The priest professed to her group being a scouting party, tasked with preventing incursions by the Dominion from the northwest. 
   Next, Lewis awakened the captain, who was unsurprisingly reticent. He quarreled briefly with Persephone before Rembrandt questioned his allegiance (“any who oppose the Rexum”). Rem went on to explain his arrangement with Zarunok, and our intent to lift the siege on Maghaven and turn the tides of the war. 
   Eventually something of an ultimatum was reached: were we to lift the siege and end Grubb’s chokehold on Maghaven, perhaps we would not seem so “full of shit” as it were. In the next breath, the captain demanded a miracle to prove we have the gods on our sides— and was quickly turned down on that front. 
   Perhaps in the end the real miracle was that the captain finally relented and offered to show us a way into Maghaven, so long as we did not kill his remaining companions. Persephone sensed even then he was holding something back on the matter, and said we should take the three prisoner.
   Eventually a different consensus was reached: we would take the captain alone as prisoner, leaving the other two to go free per his request. Lewis roused the assassin, and Rem cut her and the priest free of their bonds. 
   Persephone maintained reservations, but relented to the apparent will of the majority. The rebel trio retreated to speak privately, and I sent the spider familiar after them to listen in. The captain made an effort to talk down the other two, into accepting the situation as it was and travel away by a different route, promising in the same breath that he would kill “that woman” (presumed Persephone). 
   We learned the captain is named Harold. Harold requested supplies be returned to the remaining members of his cohort, which was granted before we went our separate ways. I requested Valindra cover our trail magically, which she did while the assassin and priest lingered to tend to their dead.

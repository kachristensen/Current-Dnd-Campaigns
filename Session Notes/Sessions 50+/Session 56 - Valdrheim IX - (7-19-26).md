## Summary
The party killed the undead dragon in the temple and other stuff. They recovered the artifacts and rested.

## IC Synopsis - Rem's Rantings
> We found ourselves in a small room with three altars after dispatching the nun-like aberrations.  The altars depicted Detarch, Etifa, and Zarunok and still had offerings on them, though it had clearly been some time since anyone prayed there.  I felt a pull from Zarunok’s altar during the battle, and offered a proper prayer afterwards.  The chains tightened around my arm in response, and I knew our path was true.  I joined Valindra at Etifa’s altar next, where she seemed worried.  I tried to reassure her before praying to Detarch and departing the altar room.  The low, draconic rumble greeted us once more as we continued to snake around the central room of the temple.  We paused outside the doors and made our final preparations before battle.  There was no monster to be seen as we entered the main chamber, but instead a massive, dark pit consumed nearly half the room.  I called out in draconic, bidding the beast to come forth and meet its doom.  I do not know if it understood my words, but the dragon-like creature crawled out of its pit just the same.
   We rushed towards the undead dragon, if it could truly be called a dragon.  It seemed more an amalgamation of people in the shape of one, with faces and limbs where scales might have been.  Regardless, our task remained unchanged.  The creature took flight out of its pit as it summoned swarms of shambling corpses to assault us.  I urged Anicca towards the maw before I channeled my power in a new manner.  Not the burning aura or the dead wings I’d manifested before, but bat-like wings akin to one of Zarunok’s devils.  Using these, I leapt from Anicca’s back and flew towards our foe.  Lewis preached to all assembled of the Living God’s virtue, and summoned a celestial defender to demonstrate his commitment.  Perse urged everyone forward towards the pit, before erecting a wall of force to cut off the first wave of swarming corpses.  The maneuver was effective, but left us grouped up near the dragon.  Only Ria was spared as the creature unleashed its frigid breath on us.  Valindra, Perse, and Anicca were all left crippled with fear, and Lewis’s concentration was broken, dismissing his celestial defender.
   Several more corpse swarms joined the fray and the situation grew dire, until Lewis rallied us once more with a massive wave of healing magic.  The dragon seemed to take offense to this, and unleashed its breath upon us once more.  Anicca was destroyed this time, but Valindra found her courage and broke free of her paralysis.  Perse floundered until Lewis fed her a potion, at which point she polymorphed the wounded Valindra into a giant ape.  Ria summoned her dragon to meet the undead one in the air, while she did her best to avoid the corpse swarms on the ground; all while hurling fire and lightning at our foes.  Wuffi, Ria’s Dragon, and I tangled with the undead dragon in the air.  My sword and their claws were ineffective, but we chipped away at the monster all the same.  I was unable to restrain the creature with Zarunok’s chains, but the chains found gaps in the armor where my sword could not.  Channeling my radiant magic through the chains proved most effective, and with the last of my energy the beast finally collapsed and fell back into its hole.  When the last of the corpse swarms were dispatched, we delved in after the dragon and found a small cavern.  More precisely, rubble-filled ruins the dragon had made its lair.  Lewis used his mace to destroy the dragon’s remains while the rest of us inspected a series of coffins.  Several were animated with magic and attacked, but we dispatched them without much trouble.  Inside the coffins we found two swords, a suit of armor, and Foshorey’s shield.
   We then ascended out of the pit and back into the temple, this time taking refuge in another altar room.  Perse erected her magical dome as we secured the room and settled in for a much needed rest.  We had retrieved both artifacts and captured Valmont’s soul per Poko Poko’s request, but the city was still plenty dangerous.  We’d return to the enigmatic lich in the morning, before making our exodus from this damned city.  As an uneasy quiet settled in, I thought of my mentor and his failed crusade once more…

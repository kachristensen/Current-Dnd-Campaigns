## IC Synopsis - Valindra
> For once it seemed our nightly rest was at ease. With the arrival of Rem's mentor Ulrich and a number of squires, our camp felt full and safe. This must have been that case, as I relaxed my body and mind, a cool relaxing sensation washed over me. A calming voice reaches me as I open my eyes into a welcoming building, and a gorgeous woman adorn with wings sitting across from me. After questioning who this woman was, she introduced herself as the goddess Etifa. Our conversation was brief and focused on my beliefs. Which of course have been lost on me for sometime. She asks what I am willing to give, and I respond as I always have, giving myself is all I have. She reaches to the heart and touches it as I come back to waking.
   Besides me, unbeknownst to me Ria had attuned herself to Cirrepti's relic. This gave her a dream as well, meeting the self same deity. Her dream much different than mine, where I felt warmth and welcome, Ria felt cold and dark. In the dark of this church Ria finds the goddess, a being wrapping in cloth and barbed wire. Ria stared at her for quite sometime not speaking. As the goddess extends her hand to Ria, she is shown a vision of her sister. Giving Ria the impression that her sister no longer lives. From Cirrepti's outstretched hand, a spider climbs down onto Ria and digs its fangs into her neck, waking her. Leaving a mark even outside her dream, and now accompanied by the same spider. 
   Rem, Ulrich and the squires all going about similar morning routines. One we have become accustomed to watching Rem each morning. Rem offers to spar with one of the squires, Bertrem, the oldest. I had hoped to speak with Rem this morning but decided to wait he was done with his sparing match, as he seemed to be enjoying himself. Rem appeared to be the superior, but Bertrem was for sure holding his own. I wonder how well I could fair against him?
   When completed I spoke with Rem regarding my encounter with Etifa. Rem was interested in the conversation but did not miss the moment to admonish me again as my wiliness put myself on the line for the others. He ended the conversation with a wide smile. Which was a bit curious to me...
   Meanwhile while we spoke, Persephone was approaching Ria about her new companion. I think fearing this was a progression on a bug connection. 
   Over the next few days we traveled to Hochstadt, which it was decided the three squires, Bertrem, Gamel and Thorkel would remain as we pressed on. Though our party caught the attention of the local authorities and we were asked to speak with military leader, Charlene March. To my surprise Rem outright told her of our intent to overthrow the Rexum. Though apprehensive to the thought, Charlene was not going to impede us directly and allow us to speak with the governor. After a night at the Omelette du Fromage, we met with Wulfrid Walstead. Rem with the silver tongue is able to talk them into our side of the new rebellion, should we succeed in Maghaven. We resupply before leaving Hochstadt. Aquiring a new saddle for Wuffi, as we were unable to recover his original one before leaving Valdrheim.
   The next morning we head out for the cliffside. The goal to use Ria and Persephone's magics to fly ourselves down the cliffside. On our approach, a group of Perytons appear in our path. Four smaller and two larger. Rem attempts to frighten them off with his new blade's light. Though remembering back from my time in the forest, I recalled these creatures being a bit too intelligent for that. I know they understand elvish, and attempt to communicate our intent to just pass through. It does not seem they listen. After casting fly on the mounts we begin to descend, only to be immediately followed. The battle began, and quite uncomfortable at that. Just as blows began to exchange with the birds, a voice rang out as a tunnel opened up into the cliff side. A large dragon appeared in the tunnel. Quite old, almost ancient. Though it seems to have seen many a battle. It's wings all but stumps on its back, and a broken horn. It seems our battle peaked its intention.

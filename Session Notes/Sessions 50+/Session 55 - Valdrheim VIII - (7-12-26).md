###### Day 33
## Summary
The party continued to fight monsters in the cave. They emerged and entered the temple to the north and fought more shit.

## IC Synopsis - Ria
> Progress through the tunnels beneath Valdrheim was again impeded by another monstrous creature in our way. Alone, it did not last long. We returned to our wanderings in short order, which inevitably led us to cross paths with yet more hostile creatures. Particularly troublesome was an encounter near a pool of rancid water, which included a froglike creature capable of spellcasting and telepathy. Following this encounter we retreated to one of the dead ends to attempt a short rest, only to be interrupted by a wandering sorrowsworn before Persephone could finish her ritual to cast the protective dome. While the creature was easily dispatched, it was… irksome. Despite minor complications we were able to rest and recover for an hour before carrying on. Closer investigation of a cave-in near our place of rest determined clearing it would take half a day without equipment or magic. We elected to seek another exit first in an effort to conserve our dwindling resources.
   Returning to the rancid pool, we discovered a sewage pipe we were able to squeeze through. We followed its course into the sewers proper, which were likewise infested with unusual creatures. By a stroke of luck we were not immediately attacked. One even spoke to us, inquiring if we wanted to join it and its companion for dinner. We were able to convince it to let us pass unimpeded, and made our way out of the sewers. Upon our emergence, we found ourselves quite close to the temple that was our destination. Rather than take on the sentries guarding the exterior immediately, we made a dash for the doors in an attempt to take cover inside before they could catch us. Unfortunately we were not as quick as we might have liked. The sentries descended upon us just as we opened the temple doors. With Persephone’s intervention we were able to somewhat ignore them and continue our advance into the temple, closing the doors behind us. Valindra got ahead of the rest of us to spot a three-headed creature deeper in, chanting unintelligibly. Rem was quick to join her, while the rest of us rushed to catch up. We were able to shake off the sentries in the process. We killed the three-headed creature in short order, and proceeded further into the temple. I will note here the strangeness of encountering so many creatures that were distinctly not undead. More singing abominations were found and dispatched the deeper we ventured into the temple.
